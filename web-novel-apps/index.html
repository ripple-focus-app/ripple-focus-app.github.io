Reading web novels until 2 a.m.

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no ads and no account, so take this with that in mind. Serial fiction apps are built so each chapter ends on a hook and the next one loads by itself. Many readers say they meant to read one chapter in bed and looked up at 2 a.m. Reading is a fine hobby, so the goal here is a time limit and a cutoff, and the app stays on the phone.

Decide how much and until when

Two numbers are enough. A daily amount, for example 45 minutes, and a time after which the novel apps stay closed, for example 23:00. Check the usage page for the last 7 days first, so the daily amount starts from what you really read now and goes down from there.

On Android (v0.1.31)

  • A daily time for each novel app. When the time is used up, the app shows the block page until the next day. Each weekday can have its own time, so Friday and Saturday can be longer.
  • If you read in several apps, one daily total can be shared by all of them, so switching apps does not reset the count.
  • A schedule that blocks the novel apps from 23:00 to 07:00.
  • Relaxing a rule waits 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or takes the unlock code. Strict mode accepts only the unlock code, at most 3 successful unlocks a day.
  • The block page has sleep sounds and slow breathing if you want something to help you fall asleep. Long playback with the screen off has not been tested on real phones yet.
  • Changing the phone clock does not bring the time back. On Android 11 and later uninstall protection can be turned on.

For a teenager who reads under the covers

On the teenager's own Android phone, set a bedtime schedule for the novel apps and a daily time that you agree on together, and keep the unlock code yourself. A reading habit is worth keeping, so a paper book at night can stay allowed. Ripple has no remote control, so rules are changed on the teenager's phone.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks chosen apps during time windows, stops deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily times, so on iPhone the bedtime window is the part that works.

What it cannot do

Ripple works on the phone it is installed on and does not filter websites, so novels read in a browser are not covered. It is not in app stores yet, so it is installed from the website.
