Reading a book without picking up the phone

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no ads and no account, so take this with that in mind. Many people who used to read a lot now open a book, read two pages and reach for the phone to check one thing. Twenty minutes later the book is still on the same page. Putting the phone in another room helps, until you need it for music, a dictionary or a message from family.

Pick a window and the apps that pull you away

Choose a fixed time, for example 21:00 to 22:00 on weekdays. Then look at the usage page for the last 7 days and pick the two or three apps you open most when you get bored, usually short videos, a feed and a forum. Only those apps get blocked. Calls, messages, the e-reader app and a dictionary keep working.

On Android (v0.1.31)

  • A schedule that blocks the chosen apps during the reading window. Each rule can apply to some weekdays only.
  • A daily time for each app if you would rather allow some scrolling, for example 30 minutes of the feed a day. Since v0.1.25 each weekday can have its own time.
  • If the distraction is inside WeChat, the WeChat Channels and WeChat mini program switches close those parts only, so chats keep working.
  • Relaxing a rule waits 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or takes the unlock code. Usually the urge passes before the wait ends.
  • Changing the phone clock does not end the window early. On Android 11 and later uninstall protection can be turned on.

For a child with reading homework

Many schools ask for 20 or 30 minutes of reading a day. On the child's own Android phone, add a window after dinner that blocks games and short videos, and keep the unlock code yourself. Agree on the time with the child first and explain that the phone decides, so the reading time is not a daily argument. Ripple has no remote control, so rules are changed on the child's phone.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks chosen apps during time windows, stops deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily times and no WeChat switches. A reading window that blocks the two apps you reach for is enough for most people.

What it cannot do

Ripple only works on the phone it is installed on and does not filter websites in the browser. It is not in app stores yet, so it is installed from the website.
