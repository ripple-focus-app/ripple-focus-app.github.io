Limiting QQ Channels and keeping QQ chats

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. In China many students use QQ for class groups and homework, and many adults use it for work groups. Blocking the whole QQ app is often not an option. The time usually goes into the Channels tab at the bottom of QQ, with game, anime and fan communities that keep scrolling.

What Ripple for Android v0.1.29 does

Version 0.1.29 adds a "QQ · Channels" item to in-app limits, under WeChat · Mini Programs. When it is on, opening the Channels tab, Popular channels, a channel chat or a channel post is blocked. QQ chats, groups and contacts keep working. You can block right away or allow a few minutes first. If you are blocked on the Channels tab, you go back to QQ Chats. If you opened a channel from another page, you go back to that page.

For yourself

Turn on QQ · Channels with a few minutes allowed, so you can check one post and then get pushed out. Making the rule looser needs a wait of 15 minutes by default (30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours can be chosen) or an unlock code, which gives the urge time to pass.

For a child

Choose block right away, keep the unlock code yourself and turn on strict mode. On Android 11 and later, Anti-Uninstall stops the app from being removed, and Ripple also resists changing the clock. The class group and private chats stay usable, so the child can still get homework messages. Tell the child which part is limited and why.

What was tested

On an OPPO phone I tested the Channels tab and opening a channel from Contacts. Both were blocked, and chats and groups were not blocked by mistake. Popular channels, channel chats and posts were only checked in tests with page samples taken from the phone. A future QQ update may need changes on my side.

iPhone

This is Android only. Ripple for iPhone is still v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It can block the whole QQ app during time windows you choose, prevent deleting apps, resist clock changes and use an unlock code. It cannot limit only the Channels part of QQ.
