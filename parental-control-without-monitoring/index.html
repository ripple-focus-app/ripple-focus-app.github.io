Parental control without monitoring?

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so read this with that in mind. Facts about Kidslox and FamiSafe come from their websites and the US App Store listing in November 2026. Prices vary by country.

What Kidslox and FamiSafe are for

Both are parental control apps. You install them on the child's device and manage it from your own phone. Kidslox works on iPhone, iPad and Android, covers up to 10 devices on one account, and has daily limits, schedules, app blocking, a web filter, instant lock, location and content monitoring. Its listing says there are no ads, it needs an internet connection, and Premium is a subscription after a 3 day trial. FamiSafe by Wondershare adds AI checks on 30+ social apps, call and message monitoring, screen viewing, location with geofencing and YouTube and TikTok history, sold as family plans.

If you need to see where your child is or what they are reading, those tools do it and Ripple does not.

A child's Android phone, limits only

Some parents only want the games and short video apps to stop after a while. Ripple sets that up on the child's phone with no account. Each app gets its own daily amount, one game can be set to 0 minutes so it stays closed all day, opens per day can be capped, and homework and bedtime schedules can be added. Turn on strict mode and keep the unlock code yourself. Anti-Uninstall comes on with it (Android 11 or later), changing the clock does not end a schedule, and a forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request. Ripple has no parent app and no remote control, so you change rules on the child's phone.

Your own phone

Parental control apps assume one person sets the rules and another lives with them. Ripple works for the phone owner too. You set your own daily amounts and schedules, and loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait or the unlock code. In strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day, so a partner or friend can set it and keep it. Quick focus locks distracting apps right away for a set time.
