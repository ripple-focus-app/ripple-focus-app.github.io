Black Friday week, and the shopping apps you keep opening

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for adults who check deals far more often than they planned, and for parents whose child browses shopping apps or tries to buy things on a shared phone.

Decide before the sale starts

Write down what you actually need before the sale week begins. Sale apps send a lot of notifications, so turning off notifications for shopping apps in system settings removes most of the reasons to open them. Turn off saved payment shortcuts if one tap checkout makes buying too easy.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.26, Android 8.0 or later) can give each app its own daily limit. You could allow 20 minutes a day for each shopping app during the sale and set the rest of the month back to 0 minutes. Since v0.1.25 a limit can also be set per weekday, so a shopping app can stay at 0 on workdays and get some time on Saturday.

If the problem is opening the app again and again, an open limit fits better. You choose how many times a day the app may open, and each open can start with a short pause of 5 to 30 seconds. That pause is often enough to notice you have nothing to buy.

Loosening any rule needs a wait of 15 minutes by default (30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours are also options) or an unlock code. Making a rule stricter works as soon as you save. Strict mode with a code kept by someone else, plus Anti-Uninstall on Android 11 and later, makes the limits hard to undo in a weak moment.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone, set shopping apps to 0 minutes and keep the unlock code yourself. If your child sometimes uses your phone, Quick Focus can lock only WeChat, payment and shopping apps before you hand it over, and the rest of the phone works as usual. Ripple blocks apps from opening. It does not control purchases inside a game, so also require a password for purchases in Google Play settings. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's phone in person.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps during a time window, prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits, no open limits and no Quick Focus. For a child, Apple's own Ask to Buy in Family Sharing covers App Store purchases.
