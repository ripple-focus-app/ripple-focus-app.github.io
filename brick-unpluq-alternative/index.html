Brick or Unpluq, or a free app?

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so read this with that in mind. Brick and Unpluq both add a small NFC device. You block apps in their app, and to get the apps back you tap your phone on the device, which works best if the device lives in another room. Facts below come from their websites and help pages in November 2026.

Brick

A battery free NFC puck listed at $59 with no subscription. Tap to start, tap again to stop. Its site says it works on Android 12 or later with NFC and on iOS 18 or later. One puck works with any number of phones, and there are five emergency unlocks if you are away from it.

Unpluq

An app with a keychain NFC tag. Its FAQ says the free version blocks two apps on one schedule. More apps, more schedules and the tag need the paid Premium. During a schedule you open a blocked app by scanning the tag or by another barrier such as shaking the phone.

Ripple

Ripple has no hardware, no ads and no account. The friction comes from a wait and an unlock code. On Android (v0.1.21, Android 8.0 or later), loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait (30 minutes for apps like Douyin) or the code. In strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day. If a friend sets the code and keeps it, that plays a similar part to leaving a puck in another room. Android also has a daily amount per app including 0 minutes, open limits, a break rule, quick focus, and blocking only WeChat Channels or Moments.

On iPhone, Ripple v0.1.5 only blocks by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code, so Brick and Unpluq do more there.

For a child's phone

A physical device only works for a child if it is kept somewhere the child cannot reach. On a child's Android phone Ripple can give game, cartoon and short video apps their own daily amounts, set one game to 0 minutes, and add homework and bedtime schedules. With strict mode on and the code kept by the parent, Anti-Uninstall turns on too (Android 11 or later), clock changes do not end a schedule, and a forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's phone.

Which one

If you want a one time purchase and a physical step to unlock, Brick fits. If you like a tag on your keys and are fine with a subscription, Unpluq fits. If you are on Android and want free per app limits and strict mode without buying anything, try Ripple.
