Your daily limit ran out, then midnight gave it back

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for people who use up their app limit by 11 pm and keep scrolling after midnight, and for parents who find their child on the phone at 1 am.

Why limits are weak after midnight

A daily limit counts by calendar day and starts over at 00:00. If you finish today's time at 11 pm, the phone treats 12:05 as a new day with a full allowance. Those late hours are when you are most tired and least likely to stop, so a daily limit is weakest right when you need it.

The late night is easier to handle with a schedule of its own. From bedtime until the next morning the chosen apps stay closed, no matter how much daily time is left.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.22, Android 8.0 or later) lets a schedule run past midnight, for example 23:00 to 07:00, with repeat days counted from the start day. During it, short video, drama and game apps will not open, and your daytime limits still work as usual. Loosening early needs a 15 minute wait by default, which you can change to 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or an unlock code. Overnight schedules show a sleep card on the block screen by default, with rain, ocean, fire or white noise and a slow breathing guide. Long playback with the screen off, incoming calls and unplugging headphones have not been tested on a real phone yet.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone, set a school night schedule such as 21:30 to 06:30, keep the unlock code yourself and turn on "unlock code only". On Android 11 or later this also turns on uninstall protection, and changing the phone's clock does not end the schedule early. Ripple has no remote management, so you set it up in person on the child's phone. Ripple needs the accessibility permission, so check it is still on after a system update.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps on a schedule, prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily time limits and no sleep sounds. If the late night is your problem, cover those hours with a Ripple schedule and leave daytime limits to Screen Time with a passcode someone else keeps.
