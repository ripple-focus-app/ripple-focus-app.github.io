Weekends that vanish into the phone

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for people who hold up fine on workdays and then lose most of Saturday and Sunday to the phone, and for parents whose child plays games from morning to night on the weekend.

Things that help before any app

Plan one thing for the weekend morning before you pick up the phone, like a walk or breakfast out. Charge the phone outside the bedroom so the day does not start in bed with a feed. For kids, agree on the weekend game time on Friday, before the weekend starts.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.26, Android 8.0 or later) lets a daily limit differ by weekday. You can give a video app 30 minutes on workdays and 1 hour on Saturday and Sunday, so the weekend has more room and still has an end. A schedule can also block apps on weekend mornings, for example 8:00 to 11:00.

If the weekend goes to many short checks, an open limit sets how many times a day an app can be opened, and each open can start with a pause of 5 to 30 seconds.

Loosening a rule needs a wait of 15 minutes by default (30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours can be chosen) or an unlock code. Making a rule stricter applies right away. Strict mode, with the code kept by someone else, and Anti-Uninstall on Android 11 and later make it harder to undo the limits on a lazy Sunday.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone or tablet, give games 0 minutes on school days and 1 or 2 hours on Saturday and Sunday, or whatever your family agrees on. A bedtime schedule keeps the weekend evening from running late. Blocked websites covers sites opened in the browser (tested mainly in Chrome). Keep the unlock code yourself. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's device in person.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps during a time window, so you can block games or video apps on weekend mornings or at bedtime. It also prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits, no weekday based limits, no open limits and no website blocking.
