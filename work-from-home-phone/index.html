Working from home and picking up the phone every few minutes

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for people who work from home and lose an hour to short checks between tasks, and for parents whose child does homework or online classes at the same table.

Things that help before any app

Put the phone in another room or face down out of reach while you work. Mute notifications from social and video apps during work hours and leave calls and your work chat on. Most of the pull comes from the phone being right there.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.26, Android 8.0 or later) can block a list of apps during a schedule, for example 9:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 18:00 on workdays. Calls, messages and the work apps you leave off the list keep working. Outside work hours, a daily limit on your main app, say 40 minutes, keeps the evening in check, and since v0.1.25 that limit can differ by weekday.

If the problem is many short checks, an open limit helps. You choose how many opens a day are allowed, and each open can start with a pause of 5 to 30 seconds. For a meeting or a block of deep work there is Quick Focus, which keeps only an allowed list of apps open for the time you pick.

Loosening a rule needs a wait of 15 minutes by default (30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours can be chosen) or an unlock code, and making a rule stricter applies right away. Strict mode with the code kept by someone else, plus Anti-Uninstall on Android 11 and later, keeps the schedule in place on a slow afternoon.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone or tablet, set a homework schedule such as 16:30 to 19:00 on school days for games and video apps, and leave class apps and the dictionary off the list. Blocked websites covers sites opened in the browser (tested mainly in Chrome). Keep the unlock code yourself. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's device in person, and working from home makes that easy to check.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps during a time window, which fits work hours and homework time well. It also prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits, no open limits, no Quick Focus and no website blocking.
