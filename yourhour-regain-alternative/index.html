What YourHour and Regain do well

YourHour has a floating timer that turns amber and then red as you near your limit, detailed reports, a short pause before an app opens, and it can block Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels while leaving the rest of each app. Its Play listing says it contains ads, with monthly and yearly Pro plans. It can also limit Android TV apps from your phone.

Regain is built around a focus timer, Pomodoro breaks and live focus rooms with other people. Its Strict Mode holds until the session ends, and it blocks Reels, Shorts and websites. Regain Pro on Google Play in India is INR 199 a month or INR 999 a year.
