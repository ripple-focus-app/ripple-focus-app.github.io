Mobile games eating your evenings, and a child who plays one more round

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for adults who sit down for one match after work and look up at midnight, and for parents who want a child's games kept to a set amount of time.

Things that help before any app

Turn off game notifications in system settings. Daily login rewards and energy timers are built to pull you back, and without the notification many of those openings never happen. Move games off the home screen into a folder, so opening one takes a small decision.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.26, Android 8.0 or later) gives each app its own daily limit, from 1 minute up to 12 hours. Give a game 45 minutes a day and it stops opening once the time is used, then resets at midnight. Since v0.1.25 the limit can differ by weekday, for example 30 minutes on workdays and 2 hours on Saturday and Sunday.

If you tend to open a game for "just one quick check" many times a day, an open limit works better. You set how many opens a day are allowed, and each open can start with a pause of 5 to 30 seconds.

A game you want to quit for a while can be set to 0 minutes so it does not open at all. Loosening any rule needs a wait of 15 minutes by default (30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours can be chosen) or an unlock code, while making a rule stricter applies as soon as you save. Strict mode with a code held by a friend, plus Anti-Uninstall on Android 11 and later, keeps the limit in place on a tired night. Changing the phone clock back does not give extra time.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone, set games to 0 minutes on school days and 1 hour on weekend days with the per weekday limit, and keep the unlock code yourself. Calls, messages, alarms and the camera keep working. Ripple controls whether an app can open. It does not stop purchases inside a game, so also turn on purchase authentication in Google Play settings. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's phone in person.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps during a time window, for example every school night from 7 pm, prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits, no per weekday limits and no open limits. For a child, Apple's Family Sharing with Ask to Buy covers App Store and in app purchases.
