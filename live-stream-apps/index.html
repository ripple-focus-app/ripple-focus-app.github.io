Watching live streams for hours every night

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no ads and no account, so take this with that in mind. A live stream never reaches a last episode. There is always one more round, one more giveaway or one more chat message, so the stream stays on long after you meant to stop. Some people also check in many times a day to see who is live.

Look at the numbers first

Open the usage page and look at the last 7 days. Two things matter: how long the live stream apps were open each day, and how many times you opened them. If the time is the problem, set a daily time. If you keep checking who is live, an open count limit helps more.

On Android (v0.1.31)

  • A daily time for each live stream app, with a different time for each weekday if you watch more on weekends.
  • A limit on how many times an app can be opened each day.
  • One daily total shared by several apps, so moving from one stream app to another keeps counting.
  • A schedule that blocks them at night, for example from 23:30 to 07:00.
  • Relaxing a rule waits 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or takes the unlock code. Strict mode accepts only the unlock code, at most 3 successful unlocks a day.
  • Changing the phone clock does not bring the time back. On Android 11 and later uninstall protection can be turned on.

For a teenager who watches game streams

On the teenager's own Android phone, give the stream apps a daily time agreed together and block them after bedtime and during homework hours, and keep the unlock code yourself. Ripple does not control payments, so tipping and in app purchases have to be handled in the app or phone payment settings. Ripple has no remote control, so rules are changed on the teenager's phone.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks chosen apps during time windows, stops deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily times and no open count limits, so on iPhone a night window is the part that works.

What it cannot do

Ripple works on the phone it is installed on and does not filter websites, so streams watched in a browser or on a TV are not covered. It is not in app stores yet, so it is installed from the website.
