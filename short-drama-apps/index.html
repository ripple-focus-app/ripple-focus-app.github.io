One more episode of a short drama

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no ads and no account, so keep that in mind. Short drama apps cut a story into episodes of a minute or two, end each one on a cliffhanger and start the next one automatically. Each episode feels too short to count, which is why a hundred of them can fill a whole evening.

Check how much it really is

Open the usage page and look at the last 7 days for the short drama apps: minutes per day and how many times they were opened. A long daily total points to a daily time. Many short visits during the day point to an open count limit.

On Android (v0.1.31)

  • A daily time for each short drama app, which can differ by weekday, so a Friday night can get more than a Tuesday.
  • A limit on how many times an app opens per day.
  • One daily total for several apps, so switching to another short drama app or a video app keeps counting.
  • A night schedule, for example 23:00 to 07:00, when the apps do not open at all.
  • Loosening a rule means waiting 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or entering the unlock code. In strict mode only the unlock code works, at most 3 successful unlocks a day.
  • Setting the phone clock back does not give the time back. Uninstall protection is available on Android 11 and later.
  • Some short dramas run inside WeChat mini programs. Ripple on Android can limit WeChat mini programs separately while WeChat chat keeps working.

For a child who watches in bed

On the child's Android phone, agree on a daily time for the short drama apps and add a night window that starts at bedtime, then keep the unlock code yourself. Many short drama apps sell episodes or memberships. Ripple does not handle payments, so turn off payments in the app or in the phone's payment settings. There is no remote control, so changes are made on the child's phone.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks chosen apps during time windows, prevents deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily times and no open count limits, so on iPhone the night window does the work.

What it cannot do

Ripple only covers the phone it is installed on and does not filter websites, so short dramas in a browser or on a tablet without Ripple are not covered. It is not in app stores yet and is installed from the website.
