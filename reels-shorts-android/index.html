Block Reels and Shorts on Android, keep DMs and normal videos

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. A lot of people do not want to delete Instagram or YouTube. Friends message on Instagram, and YouTube has tutorials and school videos. The trouble is the short video feed, where one clip turns into an hour. Parents have the same problem with a teenager who needs YouTube for homework.

What the apps give you

YouTube can remind you after some time in the Shorts feed, and Instagram has a daily time reminder. Both are reminders you can close and keep scrolling. Android's Digital Wellbeing app timer limits the whole app, so messages stop too.

Blocking only the feed on Android

Ripple for Android (v0.1.21, Android 8.0 or later) has switches at the top of the Daily time page for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and also for WeChat Channels and Moments. When one is on and you open that feed, Ripple presses back and you land where you were. Instagram chats and normal YouTube videos, search and subscriptions keep working. It uses the accessibility service to see which screen is open, so that permission has to stay on.

Turning a switch off is where most blockers fail. In Ripple, loosening any rule takes a 15 minute wait or an unlock code. If you want more than that, a daily amount for the whole app can sit on top, for example YouTube 60 minutes a day with Shorts blocked.

On a child's Android phone

Set the same switches on the child's phone and keep the unlock code yourself. With strict mode on, only the code loosens a rule, at most 3 successful uses a day, and Anti-Uninstall turns on with it on Android 11 or later. Changing the phone clock does not end a wait early. If the code is forgotten, a request lifts it after 72 hours. Ripple has no remote control from your own phone, so rules are set on the child's phone itself.

Some limits are worth knowing. On some phones the blocked screen can show for a moment before Ripple goes back. Ripple is not on Google Play, so it is installed from the APK on its website or GitHub.

On iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later) and cannot block only Reels or Shorts. It blocks whole apps during set hours, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code. So on iPhone you could block Instagram and YouTube from 21:00 to 7:00, for yourself or a child. To hide just the feeds on iPhone you need a different kind of app that opens these sites inside its own view.
