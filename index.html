Ripple guides

I make Ripple, a free app blocker with no ads and no account. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 (Android 8.0 or later) and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). Each guide covers both adults limiting their own phone and parents limiting a child's phone.

  • Brick or Unpluq, or a free app? Physical NFC phone blockers compared
  • Living alone and scrolling every evening, or raising a child mostly on your own
  • A hospital stay: scrolling in a hospital bed, staying overnight, and a child on the ward
  • Working in another city, with a child at home with grandparents
  • Video apps that pay coins for watching
  • A 30 day screen time challenge
