Putting limits on an Android tablet

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker with no ads and no account, so keep that in mind. In many homes the phone has rules and the tablet does not. A child gets the tablet for cartoons or online class and keeps watching for hours, and adults take one to bed because the bigger screen feels nicer for videos.

Tablets in Ripple v0.1.29

Ripple for Android runs on Android 8.0 and later, and that includes Android tablets. Before v0.1.29 some tablets, such as the Lenovo Y700, were treated as phones and the app said "this phone". v0.1.29 checks the system info and the size of the whole screen, so a tablet now shows as a tablet. The limits themselves work the same as on a phone. I checked the tablet detection on an emulator and have not tried it on a real tablet yet.

A child's tablet

Set a time window that blocks video and game apps during homework and after bedtime. Give video apps a daily limit, and you can pick a different limit for each weekday, for example less on school days and more on Saturday. Take a Break helps with long cartoon sessions: after 20 minutes in a row the apps close for 10 minutes. Keep the unlock code yourself and turn on strict mode. On Android 11 and later, Anti-Uninstall stops the app from being removed, and Ripple resists clock changes. There is no remote control. You set everything on the tablet itself.

Your own tablet in bed

Put the video apps under a window such as 23:30 to 07:00. If you want to stop earlier but still need a little time to finish a show, the countdown before the block starts warns you a few minutes ahead. Changing the rule late at night means waiting first, 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours if you choose a longer wait.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks apps during time windows you choose, prevents deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits and no Take a Break. I have only tested it on iPhone.

What it cannot do

Ripple limits app time on the device it is installed on. It does not filter websites in a browser, and it cannot say anything about eyesight.
