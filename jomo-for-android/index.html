Looking for Jomo on Android, or a free Clearspace alternative?

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so read this with that in mind. Facts about Jomo and Clearspace below come from their websites and US App Store listings in November 2026. Prices differ by country.

Jomo

Jomo runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac and has no Android app. It blocks apps and websites by schedule, after a limit, or until you finish an action such as a walk. The free plan gives one session, one action or limit and one screen time budget. Jomo Plus is $5.99 a month, $29.99 a year or $99.99 once, and Strict Mode needs Plus.

Clearspace

Clearspace is on iPhone (iOS 16 or later) and Android, with a Chrome extension. When your time on a limited app runs out, you can do push-ups or take a breathing break to earn more minutes, and you can start challenges with friends. The US App Store lists Premium at $6.99 a month or $44.99 a year and a Family plan at $79.99.

Ripple on your own Android phone

Ripple for Android (v0.1.21, Android 8.0 or later) is free, with no ads, no account and no subscription. Each app gets its own daily amount, such as 30 minutes of short video, and you can cap how many times a day you open it. Loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait (30 minutes for apps like Douyin) or the unlock code. In strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day, so you can ask a friend to set it and keep it. It can also block only WeChat Channels or Moments and leave chats alone. It has no push-ups, breathing screen or challenges.
