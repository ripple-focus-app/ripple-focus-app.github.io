I decide to sleep early every morning and scroll until 2am every night

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker, so keep that in mind. This page is for adults who keep making the same promise about bedtime, and for parents whose child keeps watching videos after lights out.

Set the rule in the daytime

At night you are tired, and arguing with yourself about five more minutes rarely works. Decide the end time during the day and make it awkward to change later. Charge the phone somewhere you have to get out of bed to reach, use a separate alarm clock if you can, and move bedtime earlier by half an hour at a time instead of all at once.

Android, for yourself

Ripple for Android (v0.1.24, Android 8.0 or later) lets you add a schedule such as 23:30 to 7:00. During a schedule only the apps on your allowed list open, so keep the phone, the clock and your chat app on that list and leave short video, reading and game apps off. Loosening a rule early needs a 15 minute wait by default, which you can change to 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or an unlock code. If you know you will give in, let someone at home keep the code and turn on "unlock code only", which also turns on uninstall protection. Changing the phone clock does not end the schedule.

If you are in bed, cannot scroll and still cannot sleep, the top of the schedules page has a sleep sounds and slow breathing card. Slow breathing can stop on its own after 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. Block screens also have a relax entry, and the relax page has no way back to the blocked app. Long playback with the screen off, incoming calls and unplugging headphones have not been tested on a real phone yet.

Android, for a child

On the child's phone or tablet, add a school night schedule and leave videos and games off the allowed list. Keep the unlock code yourself. Ripple has no remote management, so you set it up in person on the child's device. Ripple needs the accessibility permission, and on some phones you also need to allow it to run in the background.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later). It blocks chosen apps on a schedule, prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no sleep sounds, no slow breathing and no daily limits.

If you have trouble sleeping for weeks and feel bad during the day, see a doctor. An app can only keep the feed closed.
