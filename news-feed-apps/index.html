Refreshing news and forum apps all day

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no ads and no account, so keep that in mind. News and forum apps rarely take a long block of time. They take a minute here and two minutes there, while waiting for a kettle or between two emails, and the feed always has one more story. By evening the phone may have been unlocked for them forty times.

Count the opens first

Open the usage page and look at the last 7 days for each feed app. If the minutes are modest but the number of opens is high, the habit is checking, and a limit on opens fits better than a daily time. If both are high, use both.

On Android (v0.1.31)

  • A limit on how many times each app opens per day, for example 5 opens for a news app.
  • A daily time for each app, which can differ by weekday.
  • One shared daily total for several feed apps, so moving from the news app to the forum app keeps counting.
  • A schedule when the apps do not open at all, for example the first hour after waking up or 23:00 to 07:00.
  • Loosening a rule means waiting 15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or entering the unlock code. In strict mode only the unlock code works, at most 3 successful unlocks a day.
  • Setting the phone clock back does not give the time back. Uninstall protection is available on Android 11 and later.
  • If the articles you keep reading open inside WeChat, Ripple on Android can limit WeChat Channels and mini programs separately while WeChat chat keeps working.

For a teenager reading threads in bed

Forum threads and comment sections are easy to keep reading after lights out. On the teenager's Android phone, agree on a night window that starts at bedtime and a daily time for the forum apps, and keep the unlock code yourself. Messaging apps and calls can stay outside the rules. There is no remote control, so changes are made on the teenager's phone.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and needs iOS 16 or later. It blocks chosen apps during time windows, prevents deleting apps through Screen Time, resists clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no open count limits and no daily times, so on iPhone a morning window and a night window do the work.

What it cannot do

Ripple works on apps and does not filter websites, so the same news site in a browser is not covered unless you limit the browser too. It only covers the phone it is installed on. It is not in app stores yet and is installed from the website.
