Phone limits for a hospital stay

I am the developer of Ripple, a free app blocker. In hospital there is little to do between rounds and tests, so the phone fills the day and often the night. People staying overnight with a parent or partner scroll to stay awake. Children on the ward, or waiting hours at a clinic, get a phone or tablet and then do not want to give it back. Below is what I would set up, for your own phone and for a child's phone. Ripple for Android is v0.1.21 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5, which has fewer features.

Keep what you need

Ripple only blocks the apps you add. Leave out the phone, messages, chat apps, payment and hospital or insurance apps. Add short video, short drama, live streaming and game apps.

Your own phone

Add a night schedule such as 22:30 to 6:30. The listed apps stay closed overnight, so waking at 3 a.m. does not turn into an hour of video. Give the app that takes the afternoon a daily limit, for example 90 minutes; when the time is used up it shows the block screen until the next day. A break rule can ask for a rest after a stretch of continuous use.

If you are staying overnight with someone, the same night schedule helps. On Android Ripple can block only WeChat Channels or Moments and leave chats and calls open, so the family can still reach you.

Without strict mode, loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait (30 minutes for apps like Douyin) or the unlock code. With strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day, and a family member can keep it.

A child in hospital or at a clinic

A sick child in bed needs something to do, and a cartoon during a blood test is fine. The aim is to stop it running all day and late into the night. On Android give each video and game app a daily limit, such as an hour. A 0 minute limit keeps one app closed all day.

Add a schedule for the ward's lights out, such as 21:00 to 7:00. Calls, SMS, alarms and the camera keep working, so the child can still call you at night.

If the child knows how to change settings, turn on strict mode and keep the code yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with strict mode and needs Android 11 or later. Changing the clock does not end a schedule, and a forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request. Ripple has no remote management, so rules are set on the child's phone itself.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, can stop apps being deleted, protects against clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits or break rules, so for daily amounts use Screen Time with Family Sharing.
